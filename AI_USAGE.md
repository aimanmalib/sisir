# AI usage

I built Sisir with AI coding agents. This file says how: the workflow, what the agents generated, and what I did
myself. The numbers are from the project's own records up to 22 November 2026, when this file was written, and they
will be updated before the final push.

## Workflow

I worked with Claude models through Claude Code, in three separate roles. Each role runs as its own session and does not
share memory with the others; they talk through files in the repository and a plan.

1. **Planner.** An agent read the fest rules, the prize page, the FAQ, the official QoreChain docs and the workshop
   examples, and wrote a product requirements document: the problem, the eight rules with the official sentence each
   one comes from, what Sisir must not do, the Quantum Pass steps, the risks, and a plan cut into small slices. I read
   it and approved it before any code was written.
2. **Builder.** An agent takes the next slice in the plan, builds it, runs the related tests, and commits in small
   steps. One slice per session. It may only change the files the slice needs.
3. **Reviewer.** A separate agent, started fresh, reads the diff and the running result. It breaks the code on purpose
   (for example, removes one check from a rule) to see whether a test turns red, and looks at screenshots of the
   playground on desktop, on a 320 px screen and in WebKit on an iPhone 13 profile. It can reject the slice.

Between the builder and the reviewer there is a mechanical gate that no agent can talk its way past: the type check,
the whole test suite on a remote CI runner (vitest, Playwright and Foundry), a build of the playground, a secret scan
of the history, a limit on file length, and a check that the slice only touched files in its scope. A slice that fails
the gate or the review is not accepted, and the next session fixes it.

Rules the agents work under: testnet only; no keys or seed phrases in the repository, logs or screenshots; every number
in public text must come from a test run or from `evidence/`; Sisir is shown only on the workshop examples and on my own
code; no public post, form, payment or message to the organisers in my name.

## Generated work

Almost everything in this repository was generated by AI agents and accepted through the workflow above:

- the CLI, the rule engine and the eight rules, the text, `JSON` and `SARIF` output, and their 109 vitest tests;
- the rule fixtures, positive and negative, written for this project;
- the `SisirLedger` contract and its 14 Foundry tests;
- the playground on top of the `create-qorechain-dapp` scaffold, its 23 component tests and 4 end-to-end tests;
- the container image, deploy script, health watch and CI workflows;
- the English and Bahasa Melayu text of the playground, this README, the decision records and the other documents;
- the logo, which reuses the comb mark the builder drew for the playground, the terminal GIF, which a script records
  from the real CLI output, and the screenshots, which a script captured from the running site.

Up to 22 November 2026 the builder ran 12 sessions for 11 slices. 11 sessions were accepted on the first try. 1 was not:

| Slice | Caught by | What was wrong | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rules S005 to S008 | the reviewer | when S007 stopped asking for a changing counter in the signed message, no test turned red | a fixture whose counter is only set in the constructor, so it never changes; the same sabotage now fails ([`da5a503`](https://github.com/aimanmalib/sisir/commit/da5a503)) |

Other AI mistakes caught before they reached a judge:

- The plan expected at least three findings on the workshop draft. Sisir finds two, and both are true. The builder did
  not add or split a rule to reach the number; it recorded the measured result in
  [`docs/decisions/0002-workshop-draft-findings.md`](docs/decisions/0002-workshop-draft-findings.md).
- The first container build failed because the image left out the end-to-end folder that the playground type check
  includes ([`3b903b3`](https://github.com/aimanmalib/sisir/commit/3b903b3)).
- The web server marked a missing asset's 404 as cacheable for a year. A test that is red on the old line now guards it
  ([`4fcaae0`](https://github.com/aimanmalib/sisir/commit/4fcaae0)).

## Human contribution

I am a solo builder. These parts are mine and are not delegated to an agent:

- **Deciding.** Entering the fest with a second project, the Quantum Pass category, and approving the requirements
  document, which fixes the eight rules, their sources and what is out of scope.
- **Design direction.** I approved the visual direction of the playground before it was built.
- **Accounts and money.** Registering on the fest site, holding the test wallet, and funding the test accounts from the
  faucet. The agents never hold my login.
- **Publishing.** Creating the project page, reviewing and uploading the video, and anything posted in my name.
- **Judgement calls the agents escalate to me.** Anything about money, public wording about me, or access I have not
  given.

The narration in the video will use a clone of my own voice, made with ElevenLabs, and the video will say so.
